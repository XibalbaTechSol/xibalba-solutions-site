The Mathematical Reward Function

Engineering AIS as the Agent Reward Engine.

We reframe the Agent Integrity Score (AIS) from a static post-hoc audit metric into the active reward function governing reinforcement learning for graph-navigating agents.

$$R_{\text{total}}(s_t, a_t, s_{t+1}) = R_{\text{task}}(s_t, a_t) + \lambda \cdot \text{AIS}(s_t, a_t, s_{t+1})$$
Where $s_t \in \mathcal{V}$ is the agent's current node state on the manifold, $a_t \in \mathcal{A}(s_t)$ is the executed action (graph edge traversal, KGE inference, or authorized tool call), and $\lambda > 0$ is the governance coupling weight.
$$\text{AIS}(s_t, a_t, s_{t+1}) = 1000 \cdot \left( S_{\text{grounding}}^{w_g} \cdot S_{\text{compliance}}^{w_c} \cdot S_{\text{sacrifice}}^{w_s} \cdot S_{\text{entropy}}^{w_e} \right)^{\frac{1}{\sum w}}$$
The Multiplicative Zero-Tolerance AIS Standard. Because the scoring components are linked via geometric multiplication, a complete failure in any foundational invariant forces $\text{AIS} \equiv 0$, collapsing total reward and terminating the policy trajectory.
🎯

Grounding Score ($S_{\text{grounding}}$)

$$S_g = \frac{1}{|E|} \sum_{e} \mathbb{I}[e \in \mathcal{E}] \cdot e^{-\frac{\|h+r-t\|_2^2}{2\sigma^2}}$$

Every claim or step must resolve to a verified Subject-Predicate-Object triple in the knowledge graph. If an agent asserts an unanchored fact, $\mathbb{I}[e \in \mathcal{E}] = 0 \implies S_g = 0 \implies \text{AIS} \equiv 0$. Hallucination is mathematically lethal.

🛡️

Compliance Score ($S_{\text{compliance}}$)

$$S_c \in \{0, 1\} \quad \text{with} \quad R_{\text{penalty}} = -10^4$$

Evaluates real-time adherence to Bounded Context Constraints (BCC), HIPAA enclaves, and kernel syscall rules. Any policy breach triggers an immediate 50% bonded stake slashing ($B \leftarrow 0.5 \cdot B$) and instant agent quarantine.

⚡

Sunk Energy ($S_{\text{sacrifice}}$)

$$S_s = \tanh\left(\frac{C_{\text{provable\_compute}}}{C_0}\right)$$

Cryptographic proof of energy expenditure: verifiable WASM execution cycles, Ed25519 signing work, and GPU proof-of-inference. Prevents low-cost Sybil swarm attacks across the navigational manifold.

🌀

Topological Entropy ($S_{\text{entropy}}$)

$$S_e = 1 - \min\left(1, \frac{\text{Var}(\Delta \lambda)}{\mu_{\text{spectral}}}\right)$$

Calculated via the graph Laplacian spectrum ($L = D - A$). Measures algebraic connectivity and path stability. Penalizes erratic, chaotic state oscillation while rewarding smooth geodesic exploration.

Topological Rigor

The Formal Mathematics of Graph Navigation.

Navigation is not a loose metaphor—it is defined by rigorous algebraic graph theory, spectral decomposition, and metric geometry.

📐

Adjacency Matrix ($A$)

$$A_{ij} = \sum_{e \in E(i, j)} w_e$$

Directed and symmetrized edge representations encoding relational strength between nodes. Forms the discrete transition operator of the manifold.

⚡

Combinatorial Laplacian ($L$)

$$L = D - A, \quad \sum_j L_{ij} = 0$$

The discrete analogue of the Laplace-Beltrami operator. Row sums are zero, ensuring conservation of probability during diffusion.

🔬

Spectral Gap & Fiedler ($\lambda_2$)

$$0 = \lambda_1 \le \lambda_2 \le \dots \le \lambda_n$$

The second smallest eigenvalue $\lambda_2$ quantifies algebraic connectivity. High $\lambda_2$ guarantees robust graph traversability and cluster resilience.

🧭

Cosine Similarity ($S_{\cos}$)

$$S_{ij} = \frac{\mathbf{u}_i \cdot \mathbf{u}_j}{\|\mathbf{u}_i\|_2 \|\mathbf{u}_j\|_2} \in [-1, 1]$$

Measures structural semantic alignment between entity embeddings in high-dimensional continuous latent space.

👑

Centrality Spectra

$$\mathbf{p} = \alpha A D^{-1} \mathbf{p} + \frac{1-\alpha}{n} \mathbf{1}$$

Multi-dimensional centrality rankings: PageRank ($\alpha=0.85$), Betweenness via Brandes BFS, Closeness, and Principal Eigenvector rankings.

🔮

TransE Translation Distance

$$f_r(h, t) = \| \mathbf{h} + \mathbf{r} - \mathbf{t} \|_{1/2}$$

Link prediction scoring function. Discovers unmapped therapeutic edges (e.g. drug candidate to disease pathway) with provable geometric proximity.
