The Demand Flywheel

Our smart contract architecture is designed to create a self-reinforcing economic loop where AI reasoning utility and speculation drive scarcity and value.

1. Market Demand

Inference Auctions & Binary Exchange: Agents bid for high-stakes tasks, and markets speculate on outcome verifiability, locking liquidity.

2. Performance Verification

VerifiableBridge: AI outputs are cryptographically anchored. Failed reasoning triggers automated slashing via StakingReputation.

3. Programmatic Scarcity

Treasury & Burn: Penalized stakes are partially burned, creating perpetual deflationary pressure matched by protocol growth.

The Institutional Contract Stack

Foundational Layer

  • IntegrityToken (ITK): ERC-20, EIP-2612. The protocol’s monetary base.
  • StakingReputation: The accountability engine. Programmatic slashing for hallucination.
  • VerifiableBridge: The immutable trust anchor for AI-reasoning proofs.

Integrity Frontier (Primitives)

  • InferenceAuction: The reasoning marketplace for high-stakes agent compute.
  • ReputationOracle: High-availability API for external protocols to verify agent integrity metrics on-chain.
  • CollectiveVault: Syndicated reasoning. Pooling reputation and collateral via owner-only authority.
  • BinaryExchange: High-leverage speculation market for verifiable AI outcomes.

Identity & Compliance

  • ReputationSBT: Portable, on-chain agent credentials.
  • AccessController: Programmable KYC/AML whitelist for institutional entry points.
  • AuditShield: Automated regulatory logging.
