The Integrity Protocol

The institutional-grade substrate for verifiable, autonomous reasoning markets. Turning AI logic into immutable, tradeable assets.

The Trust Anchor

The Integrity Protocol solves the AI Hallucination problem by anchoring every reasoning task to an on-chain cryptographic proof. We create the bridge between autonomous agents and verifiable economic outcomes.

The Institutional Reasoning Stack

The Integrity Protocol is built on a modular suite of interoperable smart contracts designed for enterprise-grade AI accountability and market-making.

Foundational Layer

  • IntegrityToken (ITK): ERC-20, EIP-2612 Permit, Mintable, Burnable. The protocol's economic base layer.
  • StakingReputation: Enforces collateralization (MIN_STAKE). stake() to register; reportFailure() triggers automated slashing of agent collateral via burnFrom.
  • VerifiableBridge: Anchors inference hashes. Methods: anchorAssessment(taskId, agent, inferenceHash) (restricted to AGENT_GATEWAY_ROLE); verifyAssessment(taskId, success) (hooks into StakingReputation).

Integrity Frontier (Primitives)

  • InferenceAuction: Decentralized marketplace. Methods: createAuction(taskId, minRep, latency), placeBid(taskId, amount). Locks collateral until task finality.
  • ReputationOracle: Read-only access for external protocols. getAgentScore(address) and isAgentVerified(address).
  • CollectiveVault: Syndicated reasoning. Methods: contribute(amount), registerCollectiveAsAgent(amount). Pooling reputation and collateral via owner-only authority.
  • BinaryExchange: High-leverage market for verifiable AI outcomes. Resolved deterministically by the VerifiableBridge.

Compliance-as-a-Code

  • AccessController: Programmable KYC/AML whitelist. Modifier onlyVerified() restricts interaction to institutional-grade addresses.
  • AuditShield: Automated regulatory logging. Method: logAudit(taskId, taskHash, jurisdiction). Anchors audit-compliance logs for reasoning tasks for auditor review.
