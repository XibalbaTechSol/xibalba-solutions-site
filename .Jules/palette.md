## 2026-06-09 - [Forms Need Explicit For/Id Labeling]
**Learning:** This application's UI omitted explicit `for` attributes on labels and `id` attributes on inputs for form fields in its contact pages, making the form inaccessible to screen readers and causing loss of click area.
**Action:** Always ensure all inputs and labels are paired with explicit `for` and `id` tags to allow proper screen reader interactions and label clickability.
