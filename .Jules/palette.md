## 2026-06-05 - Form Accessibility Explicit Pairings
**Learning:** Found that implicit or visual-only form labels (lacking 'for' and 'id' pairings) were inaccessible to screen readers in contact forms, breaking a11y standards.
**Action:** Always ensure robust accessibility by explicitly pairing every <label> with its corresponding form field via matching 'for' and 'id' attributes.
