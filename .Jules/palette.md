## 2026-06-28 - [Form Input Accessibility Pattern]
**Learning:** Explicit <label for="x"> and <input id="x"> pairings are required across all application forms, as implicit wrapping or visual-only placement fails to associate inputs with labels for screen readers or allow clicking the label to focus the input.
**Action:** Always verify form inputs have unique IDs that correspond to their label's 'for' attribute, updating existing forms to comply.
